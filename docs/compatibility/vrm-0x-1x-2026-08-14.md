## Result

**Conditional.** The fixed implementation passes the local automated gate for
five VRM 0.x models and two VRM 1.0 models. Three additional local models did
not initialize within the runner's bounded 600-frame limit. The required
physical visual/control checks, 20 real-model replacements, and five-minute
SpringBone soak remain pending.

This report does not claim camera, viewport, MToon visual, GPU, macOS, or
physical SpringBone success. The official schema source is
[`vrm-specification@821c11b`](https://github.com/vrm-c/vrm-specification/tree/821c11b250d8c70d5804ee13431e42bee56ea9c0);
the reference implementation is
[`UniVRM@52e1250`](https://github.com/vrm-c/UniVRM/tree/52e1250813f370783351788b5c4cd0332e59c9c3).
